## 4. WHAT THE CURRENT App.jsx DOES (latest version)
- Top constants I can edit: PLANE_MESSAGE, PHOTOS, ABOUT_TITLE, ABOUT_TEXT, SKIN_COLOR, USE_MIXAMO (false by default).
- Entrance: a <Loader> overlay using drei's useProgress, a sunset gradient, a progress bar with stage text, and an "Enter the beach" button.
- Lake: a Three.js Water shader (extend({ Water }) from three-stdlib) with waternormals.jpg, plus a FlatWater fallback inside an error boundary (class Safe).
- Land: a big grass ground, shores on the far/left/right sides, a sand beach in front, and a wet-sand strip.
- Forest: about 80 trees placed with a seeded random generator, reusing the 3 tree GLBs, with tall ones (bigger scale) and short ones (smaller scale), far shore dense and sides lighter.
- Player: daniel.gltf (or Mixamo FBX if USE_MIXAMO=true), W A S D / arrow keys to walk relative to the camera, drag the mouse to rotate the camera, scroll to zoom, Q/E also rotate. It plays idle/walk animations by searching animation names with regex, is clamped to the beach area, and cannot walk through the reception desk.
- Billboard: two posts, a dark board with a light frame, a "MEET AHMAD" title, and three PhotoFrame components that load textures with TextureLoader (gray fallback if missing).
- Reception: a desk with a "RECEPTION" label, emma.gltf sitting behind it (lowered into the sand to hide her legs), and a paper with fake text lines.
- PaperCard: an HTML overlay with the full ABOUT_TEXT that fades in when the player is within 5 units of the desk.
- FlyingPlane: a simple plane made from primitives, with a banner (drei <Text>) that loops across the sky.
- A "Hint" overlay shows the controls after entering.
- Characters' materials: I run fixMaterials() to set metalness=0, roughness=1 and recolor any material whose name contains "skin" to SKIN_COLOR, and it console.logs every material name.
